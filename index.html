What you need

You only need two things to get started: your Canvas calendar link and the topic name you want to use in ntfy.

What you get

This project checks one Canvas calendar and sends reminders to one ntfy topic, which works well for a single student setup.

Setup flow

Three steps to start getting assignment reminders

You need your Canvas calendar link, the ntfy app, and a topic name you will subscribe to on your phone.

01

Copy your Canvas calendar link

Open Canvas, go to Calendar, and copy your calendar feed link. This is the link the app uses to look up your assignments.

https://school.instructure.com/feeds/calendar.ics?token=...
02

Install ntfy and create a topic

Download the ntfy app on your phone, choose a topic name, and subscribe to that topic in the app so your reminders show up there.

leo-canvas-alerts school-due-today stats-homework
03

Run the notifier daily

Run the notifier once a day. It checks your Canvas calendar, finds what is due soon, and sends a reminder to your phone.

Canvas calendar Daily check Phone reminder

Submit settings

Check your details before submitting

Make sure your Canvas link and topic name look right before you send them, and remember the topic name and passcode you choose in case you want to delete your account later.

Save your topic name and passcode somewhere you will remember them. You will need both later if you want to delete your account and stop notifications.

What happens after submit

The app checks that your link and topic look valid, then saves or sends them wherever you have connected this project.

This button is real. If the project is running correctly, pressing submit will actually send your settings.
