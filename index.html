Backend status

Your backend currently reads two environment variables: CANVAS_URL and NTFY_TOPIC.

Current delivery model

Right now this project is designed for one Canvas feed and one topic at a time, then posts daily reminders to ntfy.sh.

Setup flow

Three steps to start getting assignment reminders

You need your Canvas iCal link, the ntfy mobile app, and a topic name you subscribe to in the app.

01

Copy your Canvas calendar link

Open Canvas, go to Calendar, and copy the calendar feed or iCal export URL. That link is what the backend uses as CANVAS_URL.

https://school.instructure.com/feeds/calendar.ics?token=...
02

Install ntfy and create a topic

Download the ntfy app on your phone, pick a hard-to-guess topic name, and subscribe to that topic in the app. That topic becomes NTFY_TOPIC in the backend.

leo-canvas-alerts school-due-today stats-homework
03

Run the notifier daily

Run the notifier on a schedule. It checks your Canvas feed, separates anything due today, and sends the reminder to your ntfy topic.
